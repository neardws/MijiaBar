House cutaway

See the whole home. Tap what you want to change.

A pixel-art isometric cutaway sits at the top of your device list. Tap a fixture — lights, TV, air conditioner, entry light — to toggle the device it maps to.

  • Fixture by fixture.Turn on one light and only that corner lights up — not the whole room.

  • Day and night.Separate baked art for daytime and evening, following your Mac's local clock (~6:00–17:59).

  • Tap to toggle.Matched devices switch on or off right from the illustration.

  • Room to breathe.Enlarge opens a dedicated House window when you want a closer look.

Device pages

Every device looks and feels like the real thing.

Each kind of device has its own page, with an illustration that changes as the device does. Airflow follows the fan speed, bulbs glow at their color temperature, and plugs show current flowing.

  • Air conditioners. Drag to set the temperature. Mode, fan speed and swing are one click each.

  • Lights. Brightness and color temperature, with a bulb that glows to match.

  • Fans. The blades turn at the speed you set, and coast to a stop when you switch off.

  • Smart plugs. See how much power is being drawn right now.

  • Heating. Colors follow the mode — cool blue for cooling, warm orange for heating.

Live view

Check the camera without leaving the menu bar.

Live video plays right inside the device card. Pop it out into its own window when you want a closer look.

  • Only while you watch.The stream starts when you open it and stays on this Mac.

  • Nothing is recorded.Video is never saved, and your keys stay in the Keychain.

  • Off-limits to agents.The agent interface has no access to camera video.

Live view works when your Mac is on your home network.

Cloud LAN

Cloud or local network — whichever gets through.

MijiaBar reaches devices through the Xiaomi cloud and falls back to your local network when it has to. Away from home it skips the wait, and every device shows which path it took.

For AI agents

Let your AI agent run the house. On your terms.

MijiaBar comes with a command-line tool and an MCP server. Paste one message into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor and the agent sets itself up.

Setup message 
Read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/neardws/MijiaBar/main/docs/AGENT_SETUP.md and follow it to set up MijiaBar as your MCP tool.
CLI path: /Applications/MijiaBar.app/Contents/Resources/bin/mijiabar
Don't change any device's state without my consent.

This assumes MijiaBar is in Applications. The same message, with your path filled in, is in Settings → Agent interface.

You decide what it can do.

  • OffAgents can't do anything.
  • ReadDefaultList devices and read their state.
  • AllowlistControl only the devices you pick.
  • FullControl every device.

Confirm every write — each change waits for your OK in a dialog on your Mac.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download and installOpen the disk image and drag MijiaBar into Applications.

  2. Scan to sign inClick the house in the menu bar, choose your region, and scan the code with the Mi Home app.

  3. Allow local network accessWhen macOS asks the first time you control a device, click Allow so MijiaBar can reach it directly.

Questions and answers.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later — Apple silicon or Intel — and a Xiaomi account with devices in the Mi Home app.

Is MijiaBar made by Xiaomi?

No. It's an independent open-source project. It uses the same cloud API as the Mi Home app, plus the miIO protocol on your local network.

Is it safe to install?

Every release is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. The source code is public on GitHub under the MIT license.

Where does my account information go?

You sign in by scanning a QR code, so there's no password to type. Your session is stored in the macOS Keychain and is only sent to Xiaomi.

Does it work outside mainland China?

Yes. Choose your Mi Home server when you sign in: Mainland China, Europe, United States, Russia, Singapore or India.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes. Controls go through the Xiaomi cloud when your Mac isn't on your home network. Live camera view needs your home network.

Will my device work?

Most likely. Devices without a page of their own still get controls built from their MIoT spec. If something doesn't work, let us know on GitHub.

How do updates work?

From version 0.1.9 on, MijiaBar checks for updates by itself, and you can check any time in Settings. If you have 0.1.8 or earlier, download the latest version once.
